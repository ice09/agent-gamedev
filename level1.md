LOCATION: LOWER TRANSIT SECTOR

An abandoned elevated transit district deep inside the megacity.

The player traverses old railway maintenance structures suspended between
gigantic residential towers.

Environment elements:

- abandoned magnetic rail line
- massive concrete support structures
- maintenance catwalks
- broken elevators
- hanging cables
- utility pipes
- old warning signs
- drainage systems
- small improvised shelters
- distant apartments
- giant ventilation machinery
- occasional holographic maintenance interfaces

Below the playable area is an enormous vertical abyss filled with fog,
pipes, distant traffic lights and barely visible structures.

Rain enters through damaged sections of the roof.

Violet diagnostic lights are integrated into ancient machinery.

The playable path should be visually readable and relatively dark,
while the city background has enormous depth.

No characters.

16:9 composition.

Strict 2D side-scroller view.
