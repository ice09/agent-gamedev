# Player palette

Values below are transcribed from the printed color labels in
`player_master_reference.png`; they are not pixel samples of the rendered art.
Use that master sheet when other reference sheets differ.

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matte black | `#0A0A0A` | Deep fabric and darkest details |
| Charcoal | `#2A2A33` | Main clothing and armour masses |
| Gunmetal | `#4A4F5B` | Metal and cool highlights |
| Dark indigo-black | `#1B1E2E` | Indigo shadows and coat structure |
| Violet glow, primary | `#8A5CFF` | Technology strips and shoulder emblem |
| Pale lilac glow, secondary | `#D7C6FF` | Small emission highlights |
| Holographic lavender | `#B9A6FF` | Translucent geometric coat panels |
| Glass / lens | `#CFE8FF` | Restrained lens reflections |
| Skin tone, base | `#D9A98C` | Face and exposed skin |
| Skin tone, shadow | `#B8836B` | Skin shading |

Dark neutrals should occupy most of the character. Preserve the violet identity;
occasional cyan secondary accents must not replace it. The world may use rare
amber/orange lamps or windows; the character's natural skin tones remain intact.

Materials: low-specular matte tactical fabric, matte composite armour, worn
brushed metal, rubber soles, translucent holographic panels, and localized
violet/lilac emission. Readability comes from silhouette and material separation,
not from making the entire coat glow.
