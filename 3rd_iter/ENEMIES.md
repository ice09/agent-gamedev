# The infestation

Preserve the dark industrial art direction. Enemy silhouettes should be strange
and readable against the architecture; amber marks danger and mint marks repair.
These are original vector creatures, not reused humanoid opponents.

| Creature | Appearance | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rail Maw / Schienenbeißer | Six articulated legs, a suspended rib cage, a split circular jaw, and a single amber eye | Patrols a catwalk, opens its jaw to telegraph, then charges along the deck |
| Eye Jelly / Augenqualle | A floating translucent bell around a huge eye, with hanging cable tentacles | Drifts over a deck, contracts its iris, then spits an aimed slow pulse |
| Gap Choir / Lückenchor | Three detached porcelain masks suspended from a triangular frame; their mouths form black signal holes | Hovers and telegraphs a three-shot fan aimed at the player's last position |
| Null Cantor / Nullkantor | A rotating halo of masks around an enormous dark eye, with long mechanical tendrils | Final boss; alternating aimed fans, then a faster five-shot pattern below half health |

All attacks lock their direction during the warning, allowing the player to
dodge. Bodies and hostile pulses deal damage. Small health bars and hit flashes
make damage readable; defeated creatures dissolve into fragments. Avoid camera
shake and rapid whole-character flashing so the corrected motion stays clear.
